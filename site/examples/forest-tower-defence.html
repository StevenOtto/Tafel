Forest Tower Defence

Hold the forest through four seasons. Build weapons on the clearings and stop every creature before it walks, flies or swims off the map.

    Enemies

    Fish
    Swim the rivers. Armour blocks 6 damage from every hit that does not pierce it.
    Birds
    Fly above the trail. Each one that escapes costs 3 lives.
    Bugs
    Fragile, but they move fast and arrive in swarms.
